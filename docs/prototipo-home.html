How a port call works with us

Four steps, each with what you receive and when.

1Step

Nomination

Send vessel particulars, ETA and the services you need. We confirm receipt with a request number and the duty officer takes it from there.

Immediately
2Step

Proforma disbursement account

Canal tolls, reservation fee, fixed tariff, pilotage, tugs, linehandlers and every third-party cost itemized, so there are no surprises in the FDA.

Within 24 h (to confirm)
3Step

Boarding and transit

Our officer boards at the anchorage and coordinates the Authority, pilots, tugs, linehandlers, crew changes and supplies. One WhatsApp thread, one daily update.

Throughout the call
4Step

Final disbursement account

FDA with vouchers for every line. If a survey or a claim came up during the call, its report travels with it.

Within 15 days (to confirm)
