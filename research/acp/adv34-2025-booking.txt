SUBJECT:               Modifications to the Transit Reservation (Booking) System Tariffs – Cancellation fee for Long-Term
                       Slot Allocation

The Panama Canal Authority (ACP) announces modifications to the Transit Reservation (Booking) cancellation policy
under the Long-Term Slot Allocation (LoTSA 2.0) program. These changes are based on customer feedback and are
designed to encourage early cancellation requests, allowing for more efficient slot reallocation and operational
planning.

Cancellations for LoTSA 2.0 slots submitted 15 days or more prior to the required arrival date will incur a fee equal
to 80% of the awarded bid amount as per tariff 1050.IBC10. Cancellations made less than 15 days before the
required arrival date will continue to incur a fee of 100% of the awarded bid amount and will use the current tariff
1050.IBC8, described as “Cancellation of slot acquired through the Long-Term Slot Allocation method”.

Slots allocated under the LoTSA 1.0 program in 2025 will retain the current cancellation policy, including the
current cancellation fee, in accordance with the established terms and conditions, until the end of their validity
period.
