## 6. Guía pilar del Canal (EN, v0 con cifras oficiales)

**Title:** Panama Canal transit cost, tolls and booking: the 2026 guide for shipowners and operators
**Es la página `/panama-canal-transit-guide`, no un post.** Regla de publicación (auditoría comercial): **no publicar un tarifario propio.** La guía explica de qué se compone el costo, muestra **dos ejemplos resueltos y fechados a partir de PDAs reales anonimizadas** (bulk carrier Panamax de 35.000 DWT cargado, tránsito sur; tanquero de productos Neopanamax) y enlaza al Maritime Tariff y al OP Notice vigentes. Toda cifra que sobreviva la revisa Guillermo línea por línea contra el tarifario, no contra prensa, y lleva el sello *"Figures verified against the ACP tariff by Capt. Guillermo A. Peña on {fecha}"*. Las tablas de abajo son material de trabajo interno, no texto final.
**TL;DR:** A Panama Canal transit is priced in three layers: the toll (by vessel segment and size), the fixed and ancillary fees (security, inspection, pilotage, tugs, linehandlers) and the scheduling layer (reservation fee, or auction premium for last-minute slots). Booking periods and fees are set by the ACP's OP Notice to Shipping in force (N-7-2026 at the time of writing). Your agent calls the funds ahead of transit (the ACP requires them 48 hours before), files the pre-arrival package through the ACP electronic system (EDCS) at least 96 hours ahead and turns the estimate into a PDA. Last reviewed 28 August 2026. Sources: Panama Canal Authority, Maritime Tariff List and OP Notice to Shipping N-7-2026.

**Section 1: What a transit costs (components, with two worked examples)** (material interno: ACP Maritime Tariff List, leído el 28-ago-2026; verificar contra el tarifario vigente antes de publicar; los portacontenedores pagan capacidad (TTA) + TEU cargados (TTL) + vacíos (TTE), y LNG/LPG van por tramos de m³, así que ninguna cifra única es correcta sin ese detalle)

Neopanamax tug packages are higher (quote in PDA).

**Section 3: Booking, periods and fees** (OP Notice N-7-2026 and Maritime Tariff, effective 1-Jan-2025)
- Booking periods: First period 90 to 15 days before transit for the Panamax locks (90 to 31 days for Neopanamax); Period 1.a 30 to 15 days (Neopanamax only); Second period 14 to 8 days; Third period 7 to 2 days, closing at 15:00.
- Reservation fees: Regular $12,000 · Super $50,000 · Neopanamax $100,000. Last-minute reservation: $25,000 / $100,000 / $200,000. Daylight transit reservation (regular vessel) $30,000. Cancellation charges 50% to 100% of the reservation fee depending on notice.
- LoTSA: long-term slot allocation for Neopanamax through sealed-bid auction packages; unallocated slots roll to Period 2. Auction premiums for unreserved slots vary with demand; the widely reported record was about USD 4 million (USD 3.975 M) in November 2023 during the drought (ACP/press; cite with link). Cualquier cifra de 2026 solo con fuente enlazada.
- Just-in-Time (JIT) transit: up to 4 JIT slots per day in the Panamax locks, 1 per direction in the Neopanamax locks (N-7-2026). Required arrival for booked Neopanamax and Panamax Plus vessels: 22:00 the day before the booked date (02:00 on the booked date for Neopanamax LNG carriers) (N-7-2026; Guillermo verifica contra el aviso vigente).

**Section 4: Requirements and documents.** Pre-arrival package through the ACP electronic pre-arrival system (EDCS) at least 96 hours before arrival (vessel particulars, certificates, crew list, PCSOPEP for vessels carrying 400 t or more of oil with a Panama-resident authorized person, deficiencies matrix).
