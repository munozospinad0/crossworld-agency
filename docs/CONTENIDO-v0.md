## 6. Guía pilar del Canal (EN, v0 con cifras oficiales)

**Title:** Panama Canal transit cost, tolls and booking: the 2026 guide for shipowners and operators
**TL;DR:** A Panama Canal transit is priced in three layers: the toll (by vessel segment and size), the fixed and ancillary fees (security, inspection, pilotage, tugs, linehandlers) and the scheduling layer (reservation fee, or auction premium for last-minute slots). Booking opens 90 days before transit and closes 2 days before. Your agent guarantees the funds, files the pre-arrival package 96 hours ahead and turns the estimate into a PDA. Last reviewed 28 August 2026. Sources: Panama Canal Authority, Maritime Tariff List and OP Notice to Shipping N-7-2026.

**Section 1: How tolls are calculated** (source: ACP Maritime Tariff List, read 28-Aug-2026; verify before publishing)

| Segment | Regular | Super | Neopanamax |
|---|---|---|---|
| Containers, loaded (per TEU) | $35 | $35 | $35 (<10k TEU) / $45 (≥10k TEU) |
| Containers, empty (per TEU) | $6 | $6 | $6 |
| Dry bulk (per DWT) | $1.65 | $1.50 | $0.80 |
| Tankers (per PC/UMS ton) | $6.00 | $5.25 | $3.25 |
| LPG carriers (per m³) | $3.50 | $3.85 | $2.75 |
| LNG carriers (per m³) | $3.50 | $3.85 | $2.05 |
| Vehicle carriers / RoRo (per PC/UMS) | $6.00 | $4.75 | $2.75 |
| Passenger vessels (per PC/UMS) | $4.25 (<10k) / $5.00 | $5.50 | $5.50 |
| General cargo (per PC/UMS) | $3.50 (<10k) / $3.25 | $3.00 | $1.50 |

Plus a **fixed tariff per transit** (effective 1-Jan-2024): regular vessel under 7,500 PC/UMS $15,000; container, tanker and specialty over 7,500 PC/UMS $60,000; Super $100,000; Neopanamax under 10,000 TEU $200,000; Neopanamax 10,000 TEU and above $300,000. (Press reports mention a toll freeze until 30 September 2026; confirm with the ACP before publishing.)

**Section 2: Fixed and ancillary fees** (ACP, effective dates 2024 to 2025)
Security charge $165 (under 3,000 PC/UMS) or $1,250 · Inspection $120 (small vessels) or $670 (standard) · Pilotage: transit delay $550 to $800, port pilotage $725 to $3,700 · Tugs, Panamax locks: complete transit large vessel $11,600; partial $3,700 to $9,360 · Linehandlers: $270 (Panamax) or $325 (Neopanamax) per handler for a complete transit. Neopanamax tug packages are higher (quote in PDA).

**Section 3: Booking, periods and fees** (OP Notice N-7-2026 and Maritime Tariff, effective 1-Jan-2025)
- Booking periods: First period 90 to 15 days before transit for the Panamax locks (90 to 31 days for Neopanamax); Period 1.a 30 to 15 days (Neopanamax only); Second period 14 to 8 days; Third period 7 to 2 days, closing at 15:00.
- Reservation fees: Regular $12,000 · Super $50,000 · Neopanamax $100,000. Last-minute reservation: $25,000 / $100,000 / $200,000. Daylight transit reservation (regular vessel) $30,000. Cancellation charges 50% to 100% of the reservation fee depending on notice.
- LoTSA: long-term slot allocation for Neopanamax through sealed-bid auction packages; unallocated slots roll to Period 2. Auction premiums for unreserved Neopanamax slots exceeded $400,000 in mid-2026 and one slot reached about $4 million in May 2026 (press; cite gCaptain/ACP).
- Just-in-Time (JIT) transit: up to 4 JIT slots per day in the Panamax locks, 1 per direction in the Neopanamax locks. Required arrival for booked Neopanamax and Panamax Plus vessels: 22:00 the day before the booked date (02:00 on the booked date for Neopanamax LNG carriers).

**Section 4: Requirements and documents.** Pre-arrival package to the ACP maritime portal at least 96 hours before arrival (vessel particulars, certificates, crew list, PCSOPEP for vessels carrying 400 t or more of oil with a Panama-resident authorized person, deficiencies matrix). Missing PCSOPEP means no transit.
**Section 5: Draft, restrictions and water levels.** Current maximum draft (dato con fecha) · Neopanamax beam threshold: over 107 ft (32.61 m) beam or over 966 ft length → Neopanamax locks · Advisories (link).
**Section 6: What your agent does and what a PDA includes.** Sample PDA: tolls, reservation fee, fixed tariff, security, inspection, pilotage, tugs, linehandlers, launch, agency fee, bank charges, contingencies; funds guaranteed 48 hours before; FDA with vouchers.
**Section 7: Common mistakes.** Late nomination, wrong PC/UMS assumptions, no PCSOPEP authorized person, unfunded PDA, crew change without immigration lead time.
